About the council

The Nepal Pharmacy Council was established under the Nepal Pharmacy Council Act, 2057 BS (2000 AD). It registers pharmacists and pharmacist assistants, sets minimum standards for pharmacy education, and keeps the professional register that protects the public from unqualified practice.
