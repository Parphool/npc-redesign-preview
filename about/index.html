About the council

The Nepal Pharmacy Council (NPC) is the statutory body that registers and regulates pharmacy professionals in Nepal under the Nepal Pharmacy Council Act, 2057 BS.

What the council does

  • Keeps the register of pharmacists and pharmacist assistants allowed to practise in Nepal
  • Runs the name-registration (licensing) exam and publishes results
  • Sets minimum requirements for diploma and bachelor pharmacy programmes
  • Issues Letters of Good Standing and No Objection Certificates
  • Runs continuing professional development (CPD) activities

History

The Nepal Pharmaceutical Association (NPA) played a pivotal role in forming the council. Work on a draft NPC Act gained momentum after the National Drug Policy 1995 (2051 BS), whose policy strategy (Section 4.4c) called for legislation to register people working in the pharmacy profession.

The NPA’s draft was discussed at the national seminar “Need of Pharmacy Council for the Development of Pharmacy Services in Nepal” on 10 April 1997 (28 Chaitra 2053), during the NPA’s silver jubilee. The seminar’s recommended draft went to the Ministry of Health and Population, and it became the Nepal Pharmacy Council Act, 2057.
